      Zoom Meeting

      Book a Zoom Meeting with your teacher.

      Teachers open their available times first. You pick a slot that suits you, book your seat, and your Zoom Meeting link appears — with a one-click add to your calendar.

      • Teachers publish their real availability — you only ever see times that can actually be booked.
      • Every seat is counted, so a session is never overbooked.
      • Your Zoom Meeting link unlocks the moment you book, and stays private until then.
      • Times show in your own time zone, wherever you are.
