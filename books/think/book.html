How to use this book

Every unit has 4 pages
1Look and think

Look at the picture and answer the questions.

2Talk about it

Describe the picture in full sentences.

3Sentence patterns

Learn two patterns and make new sentences.

4Let's practise

Order the words, write and draw.

Scan & listen

Open the camera on your phone and point it at the QR code. You will hear everything on the page in natural British English.

Tip for parents: listen together, repeat every sentence, then practise with your child every day.
